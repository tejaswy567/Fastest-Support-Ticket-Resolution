Definitions and data notes
  • Handoff: each time the ticket's resolver group or analyst changed (the reassignment_count field).
  • Met target: the made_sla field, read as “met the target”. Tickets marked true were resolved in a median of about 1 hour, against about 200 hours for false.
  • Waited for user info: the ticket entered the Awaiting User Info status at least once. Only resolved tickets are counted, so the figure is a little higher than the 22% of all tickets quoted in the case study.
  • Median, not average: resolution times are heavily skewed, so medians describe a typical ticket better.
  • Group 70 is likely the frontline service desk. Groups and categories are anonymized codes. Unassigned means the group was blank.
  • Targets on the cards are proposals from the case study, still to agree with a sponsor.
  • Data: Amaral, C., Fantinato, M. and Peres, S. (2018) Incident management process enriched event log. UCI Machine Learning Repository, CC BY 4.0. https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/498/incident+management+process+enriched+event+log. About 6% of tickets have no resolution date and are left out.
  • Portfolio case study by Tejaswy Thonda. This file works offline: all data is inside it.
